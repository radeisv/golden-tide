# Asset credits — Golden Tide

## Sample photos (app/samples/)

All six were made for this entry with an image model (Muse media pipeline),
photorealistic golden-hour coastal scenes. No real person, no scraped photo,
no game artwork.

- `01-hero.jpg` — laughing woman with curly hair and sunglasses, golden-hour beach
- `02-bar.jpg` — blonde woman at a neon beach bar at dusk
- `03-marina.jpg` — silver-haired man in a light suit at a marina, city skyline
- `04-moto.jpg` — man on a motorcycle, coastal road at sunset, palm trees
- `05-boat.jpg` — couple laughing on a boat, sun flare
- `06-car.jpg` — red sports car at a sunset pier, man leaning on it
